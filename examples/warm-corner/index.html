Two paragraphs about us

We opened in 2019 in an old workshop: we kept the brick, added light and large tables. One of the founders is always behind the counter — if the coffee tastes off, say so and we will brew it again.

On Thursdays we run free tastings of the new roast, on weekends we bring out board games. Dogs are welcome and the Wi-Fi password is on the wall.
